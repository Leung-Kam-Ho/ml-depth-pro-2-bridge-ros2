Method

Our depth estimation network consists of a multi-view transformer that ingests one or multiple input frames and processes them jointly, and a DPT decoder, which predicts an inverse depth map per input frame. 1 The transformer shares information across frames: after the first third of its blocks, it alternates attention within each frame and attention across frames. 2 The DPT decoder takes tokens from several transformer blocks, assembles them into feature maps at 1/32 to 1/4 of the input resolution, and decodes them into an inverse depth map per frame.
